Ironman Dashboard — Privacy Policy

Ironman Dashboard is a personal training dashboard used by a single athlete and their coach. It is not a public service.

Data accessed

With the athlete's consent, the app reads sleep, heart rate, heart-rate variability and exercise data from the Google Health API (read-only), along with workouts from Garmin Connect and the training plan from TrainingPeaks.

How it is used

The data is used only to show training load, recovery and sleep to the athlete and their coach inside this dashboard. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

Storage

Data is stored in a private database on the athlete's own server and is accessible only after logging in. Access to Google data can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored data is deleted on request.

Contact

Questions: contact the dashboard owner through the Google Cloud project's support email.
